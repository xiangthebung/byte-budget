# Byte Budget Plus — subscription and refund terms

Effective: 9 August 2026

Byte Budget Plus is sold by Byte Budget's developer, reachable at
xiangli3625@gmail.com. Google is not the seller and does not process the transaction.
ExtensionPay provides the account and checkout pages and Stripe processes payment.

## Plans and renewal

- Monthly: **CA$0.99 per month (CAD)**.
- Yearly: **CA$7.99 per year (CAD)**.
- Trial: **14 days**, with no charge from Byte Budget. A trial does not itself begin a
  paid subscription.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically at the selected interval until
cancelled. The checkout page shows the selected plan, currency and amount before the
purchase is confirmed.

## Cancellation

The **Manage subscription** button in Byte Budget opens the ExtensionPay account page.
Cancelling stops future renewals. Plus access continues through the end of the period
already paid for. Cancelling does not delete settings or recorded usage.

## Refunds

For a billing error or refund request, email xiangli3625@gmail.com within 14 days of the
charge and include enough information to identify the ExtensionPay account. Requests
are reviewed individually. This policy does not limit rights that consumer law gives
you in your country or region.

## What is being sold

Measurement, the core dashboard, daily limits and the standard Data Saver levels are
available without payment. Plus unlocks the additional controls and reporting depth
listed in the extension before checkout. The current free/Plus boundary is also
