      Privacy & data

      Every usage figure stays in this browser profile. If you connect a Plus account, Byte Budget also checks its status with ExtensionPay.

      What is recorded

      Byte Budget records bytes per site per day and per hour, which hosts a site's bytes came from, and one row per page load holding the site and origin — never a path or a query string. None of that measured data is uploaded. Preferences follow you through Chrome sync; site names, limits and exceptions do not leave this profile. A connected Plus account is checked separately with ExtensionPay, as disclosed in the Plus section. The figures cover this Chrome profile on this device: other profiles, browsers, devices and everything outside Chrome are invisible.

      Export

      One row per site per day, as stored: the date, the site, bytes down and up, requests, and how much of the down figure was estimated rather than measured.

      What Plus unlocks

        What the subscription check sends

        A never-connected free install makes no subscription request. Starting a trial, subscribing or restoring creates an opaque account key stored on this device. Byte Budget sends that key to extensionpay.com about four times a day; it never sends usage, site names, limits or settings. The reply can contain an account email and subscription details. Byte Budget keeps only the paid state, dates and plan interval and discards the rest. Card details stay on ExtensionPay and Stripe's pages.
